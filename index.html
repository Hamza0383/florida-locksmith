Locks Have a Hard Life Around Here

Salt air is the big one. If you live anywhere near the ocean, from Amelia Island down to St. Augustine Beach, your front door lock is slowly corroding. Key fobs suffer too. People often ask us to rekey a sticky lock, and sometimes that's fine. But if the inside is eaten up, a rekey just puts new pins in a bad lock. We'll tell you when buying a new one makes more sense.

Then there are the old houses. Parts of St. Augustine, downtown Fernandina and Riverside in Jacksonville still have mortise locks and door sizes that a big-box deadbolt simply won't fit. Those jobs need patience. Forcing it ruins a door that can't easily be replaced.

Rentals keep us busy as well. The beach towns and St. Johns County are full of vacation homes and long-term rentals, and owners want the locks changed every time a tenant leaves. A lot of them have switched to keypad locks so they can change the code instead.

And cars get locked up everywhere. Beach parking, hotel garages, a gas station off I-95. We come to the car, so you don't pay for a tow.

What We Do

For Your Home

Lockouts at houses, apartments and condos, mostly opened without a scratch. We also do rekeying and lock changes, new deadbolts, lock repair, spare keys and smart locks.

Just bought a place? Get the outside locks rekeyed. You have no idea how many copies the old owners handed out, and it costs a lot less than new hardware. See house lockouts or everything on our residential page.

For Your Car

We get you back in, and we make keys on the spot. That covers plain keys, chip keys, remote-head keys, fobs and a lot of push-to-start smart keys. Lost every copy? Often we can still help, depending on the vehicle.

To be honest, a few newer models need software only the dealer has. If yours is one, you'll hear it from us on the phone, not after we've driven out. Bring your registration or title. Details: car lockouts, car key replacement, key fob programming, ignition repair and our automotive page.

For Your Business

Offices, shops, restaurants, warehouses, clinics and apartment buildings. We change and rekey locks, fix doors that won't latch, and set up master key systems so staff only get into the rooms they need. If you want tighter control, ask about high-security locks or access control.

Managing a handful of properties? Our property management service handles turnovers without you chasing three different locksmiths. Full list on the commercial page.

Safes

Forgot the combination? Keypad died? Bolt jammed? We'll try the gentlest way in first. We also install safes and bolt them down properly. See safe services.
