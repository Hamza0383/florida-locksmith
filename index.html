What We Do

What a Jacksonville Locksmith Can Do for You

Most of our calls come from three kinds of customers. Homeowners need help with lockouts, rekeying after a move, new deadbolts, or a smart lock. Drivers call when the keys are locked inside, lost, or a key fob quits working. Business owners need lock changes, master key systems, or a door that won't latch at closing time.

We also open and repair safes, pull broken keys out of locks and ignitions, and secure doors after a break-in.

Here's what we handle week in and week out:

  • House, car, and business lockouts
  • Lock rekeying and lock replacement
  • Deadbolt installation and door lock repair
  • Car key replacement and key fob programming
  • Transponder and remote key programming
  • Broken key extraction
  • Smart lock installation
  • Safe opening and repair
  • Master key systems and commercial door hardware
The Process

How a Service Call Works

1

Tell us where you are

Give us the address or parking spot, whether it's a home, vehicle, or business, and what's going on. For a car, have the year, make, and model handy.

2

We check the problem

The technician looks at the lock, key, door, or ignition and works out what it really needs: a repair, a rekey, a new lock, or a programmed key.

3

You hear the price first

We explain what we recommend and what it costs before we touch anything.

4

We do the job

We use entry tools made for your type of lock or vehicle and work to get you in without damage.

5

We test it before we leave

New keys get tried in every lock. Programmed keys and fobs get tested on the ignition and door locks.

Residential Locksmith Services

We work on houses, apartments, condos, garages, and rental homes across Jacksonville. Common jobs include house lockouts, rekeying, deadbolt installation, door lock repair, key copies, and smart lock setup. Just bought a home? Rekeying every exterior door is one of the first things we recommend, since you never know how many copies of the old keys exist. Landlords and property managers call us between tenants for the same reason.

Car Locksmith Services

Keys locked in the car, keys lost, or a fob that won't respond, we handle all of it on site. We cut and program replacement keys for most makes and models, including standard metal keys, transponder chip keys, remote head keys, and many smart keys. We also get broken keys out of doors and ignitions. In many cases we can make a new key even if you've lost every copy, depending on the vehicle's year, make, and security system. Expect to show proof that the car is yours.

Commercial Locksmith Services

We handle business lockouts, commercial lock replacement, rekeying after staff changes, master key systems, door hardware repair, and access control upgrades. A master key system lets managers open every door while each employee only opens the doors they need.

Safe Services
