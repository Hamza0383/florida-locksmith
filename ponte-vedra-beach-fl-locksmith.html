Why Ponte Vedra Homeowners Choose North Florida Locksmith

We treat premium hardware with care

Custom doors and high-end handlesets aren't the place for guesswork. We identify your hardware, take careful measurements, and confirm compatibility before installing anything, so the new hardware fits the existing door prep without unnecessary drilling.

Coordinated looks across your home

Want your front, back, and garage entry hardware to match in finish and style, and work on one key? We can plan that with you.

Security upgrades that make sense

High-security cylinders give you key control, so no one can copy your key at a hardware store. We pair them with solid strike plates and proper installation, since a lock is only as strong as the door around it.

Smart access for busy households and second homes

Give the housekeeper, pool service, or visiting family their own codes, schedule when they work, and delete them when they're no longer needed. If you split your time between homes, manage access from your phone.

Coastal-ready recommendations

Salt air along A1A is hard on exterior hardware. We'll point you toward finishes and materials that hold up better near the ocean.
