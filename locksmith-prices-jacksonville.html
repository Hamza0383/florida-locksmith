What Affects Locksmith Prices in Jacksonville

  • Service call or trip fee. Covers getting a technician and equipment to you. Some locksmiths fold it into the job price, others list it separately. Always ask.
  • Labor. Simple jobs take minutes. Commercial hardware, high-security locks, and electronic systems take more time and skill.
  • Parts and hardware. New locks, cylinders, key blanks, fobs, and smart locks all add to the total.
  • Number of locks. Rekeying is usually priced per cylinder, so a house with six cylinders costs more than one with two.
  • Programming. Transponder keys, fobs, smart keys, and smart locks need programming equipment and time.
  • Time of day. Nights, weekends, and holidays often cost more because a technician has to be available around the clock.
  • Location. Distance from our base can affect travel charges.

Typical Costs by Service

🏠

House lockout

Depends on your lock, the entry method, location, and time of day. A standard deadbolt costs less to open than a high-security or smart lock. Typical range: $65–$150.

🚗

Car lockout

How much to unlock a car in Jacksonville depends on your vehicle, its lock system, where you are, and the time. Give us your year, make, and model for an accurate quote. Typical range: $65–$150.

🔑

Car key replacement

Usually the widest price range. A basic metal key costs the least. Transponder keys add programming. Remote keys and push-to-start smart keys cost the most. OEM keys cost more than aftermarket ones. Typical range: $120–$450, with push-to-start smart keys at the high end.

🔄

Rekeying a house

Priced mainly by the number of cylinders. Count every lock that takes a key, including front, back, side, and garage entry doors, and multiply by the per-cylinder price. Rekeying is almost always cheaper than replacing locks that still work. Typical range: $25–$40 per cylinder.

📱

Smart lock installation

Depends on the lock model, your existing door hardware, and whether the door needs adjustment. The lock itself is usually the biggest cost. Installation typically runs $85–$150, plus the lock ($120–$350 for most models).

🏢

Commercial work

Depends on the hardware, number of doors, and what's involved. Panic bars, master key systems, and access control are priced per project. Most service calls start around $95; larger jobs are quoted after a site visit.

What Upfront Pricing Should Look Like

A good quote tells you:

  • What work will be done
  • The service call or trip fee, if any
  • Labor
  • Parts or hardware
  • Programming, if needed
  • Any after-hours charge
  • The expected total

If anything changes once the technician sees the lock, they should tell you and get your approval before doing extra work. That's how we work at North Florida Locksmith.

How to Avoid Locksmith Scams in Jacksonville

Locksmith scams are common nationwide. The most frequent is bait-and-switch pricing: an ad promises "$19 service" and the bill ends up at $300 or more. Protect yourself:

  • Ask for the total price on the phone, not just a "starting at" number.
  • Ask whether there's a trip or service call fee.
  • Ask about parts. Are keys, cylinders, or programming included?
  • Ask about after-hours charges if it's late or a weekend.
  • Get the business name and check that it has a real local presence and reviews.
  • Be wary of anyone who wants to drill a standard lock right away. Most lockouts don't need drilling.
  • Confirm the price again before work starts, and don't sign a blank form.
