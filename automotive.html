Our Automotive Services in Detail

🔒

Car lockouts

Keys inside the car or locked in the trunk? We use entry tools made for your vehicle to get you in without scratching paint or damaging the door, seals, or electronics.

🔑

Car key replacement

Lost, stolen, or broken keys? We cut and program a new one. In many cases we can make a key with no original, using your vehicle information and proof of ownership.

📱

Key fob replacement and programming

Fob lost, cracked, or no longer working? We supply a compatible replacement and program it to your car. Some vehicles need special diagnostic equipment, which we'll tell you about upfront.

🔧

Transponder keys

Transponder keys have a chip the car must recognize before it will start. We cut the blade and program the chip to your immobilizer.

⚙

Ignition repair

If your key sticks, won't turn, or the ignition feels loose, the cylinder may be worn. We diagnose the problem and repair or replace the ignition cylinder, then match it to your key.

🔄

Broken key extraction

A key snapped in the door or ignition? We remove the broken piece without damaging the lock and make you a new key.
