How Our Car Unlock Service Works

1

Call with your details

Tell us where the car is, its year, make, and model, and where the keys are: inside the cabin, in the trunk, in the ignition, or gone.

2

Get a price and arrival time

We quote the job and give you an estimated arrival before we send anyone.

3

Show proof it's your car

Have your ID and registration, or something showing you're authorized to use the vehicle. This protects you as much as anyone.

4

We pick the right entry method

Every car is built differently, so the technician checks your door design and lock system first. We use professional tools like long-reach tools, wedges, and inflatable air wedges. The old slim jim only suits some older vehicles and can damage wiring in newer ones, so we rarely use it.

5

We open the car

No forcing, no broken glass. Our goal is to leave your door, seals, and paint exactly as we found them.

6

We get your keys and check the locks

Once you're in, we make sure the doors lock and unlock properly before we go.

Lockout Situations, Explained

🔒

Keys locked inside

The most common call we get. We open the door, you grab your keys, and you're on your way.

🚗

Keys locked in the trunk

Some trunks can be reached from the back seat, others can't. We check how your vehicle is built and choose the simplest way in, whether through the cabin or the trunk itself.

🔑

Keys in the ignition

Many cars lock automatically, sometimes with the engine running. We get you in before you burn through a tank of gas.

📱

Push-to-start and smart key cars

These vehicles use electronic fobs and proximity sensors, so they need a different approach than older cars. Give us the full vehicle details and we'll bring the right tools.

🔄

Lost or broken keys

If your keys are gone or snapped, opening the door is only step one. We can cut and program a new key on site for most makes and models, so you can drive away the same visit.
