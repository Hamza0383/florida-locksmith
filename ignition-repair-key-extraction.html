Common Ignition Problems

🔑

Key won't turn

Usually caused by a worn key, worn wafers inside the cylinder, debris, or the steering lock being engaged. Before calling, try gently turning the steering wheel left and right while turning the key. If the steering lock was holding it, the key should release. Don't force it, since a bent or broken key makes the job bigger.

🔒

Key stuck in the ignition

Common causes include steering-lock pressure, the car not being fully in Park, a worn key, or damaged wafers. Make sure the shifter is all the way in Park and try the steering wheel trick above. On cars with an electronic shift lock, a stuck key can also point to an electrical issue that needs further diagnosis.

🔧

Key broke off in the ignition or door

Don't try to dig the broken piece out with tweezers or a pin. You'll usually push it deeper. We use extraction tools made for this, check the lock for damage afterward, and cut you a new key.

🚗

Car won't start because the key won't turn

If the key won't rotate, the problem is usually mechanical: the key, the cylinder, or the steering lock. We check all three and tell you whether it's something we can fix or something a mechanic needs to look at.

⚙

Worn ignition cylinder

Years of use wear down the parts inside the cylinder. If it's too far gone to repair, we replace it and match it to your key, or rekey it to work with a new one.

Locksmith or Mechanic?

Call a locksmith when the problem is the key or ignition lock: a key that won't turn, a stuck or broken key, or a worn cylinder. Call a mechanic when the key turns fine but the car won't crank or start, which points to the battery, starter, ignition switch wiring, fuel, or engine. If you're not sure, call us. We'll tell you honestly if it's outside our work.
