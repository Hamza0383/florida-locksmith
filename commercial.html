Master Key Systems

A master key system lets each person carry one key that opens exactly the doors they need. For example:

  • Key A opens the front office.
  • Key B opens the stockroom.
  • Key C opens the break room.
  • The master key opens all three.

Owners get a master key, managers get keys for their department, and staff get keys for their own areas. We design the system around your building and your team, and can often use your existing locks by rekeying them. If some locks aren't compatible, we'll replace only those cylinders.

Panic Bars and Exit Devices

Panic bars, also called push bars or exit devices, let people get out quickly in an emergency while keeping the door locked from outside. When they stop latching, stick, or come loose, both security and safety suffer. We repair push bars, adjust latches and strikes, tighten mounting hardware, and replace devices that can't be saved. If an exit device needs a part we don't have on hand, we'll tell you how long it will take to get.

Door Closer Repair

A closer controls how a commercial door swings shut. Signs it needs attention:

  • The door slams or closes too fast
  • The door closes too slowly or stays open
  • The door doesn't latch
  • The closer is leaking oil
  • The arm or mounting is loose

We check the closer and the door's alignment, then adjust or replace it.

Storefront, Mortise, and Rim Cylinder Service

Glass storefront doors usually use narrow-stile locks, mortise cylinders, or rim cylinders. These differ from residential hardware and need the right parts. We repair, rekey, and replace storefront locks, mortise lock bodies, and rim cylinders. We also install ADA-friendly levers that are easier to open than round knobs.

How Often Should a Business Rekey?

Rekey whenever someone who shouldn't have a key might still have one. Common triggers:

  • An employee is let go or quits
  • Keys are lost or stolen
  • A manager or tenant changes
  • After a break-in or security incident
  • When moving into a new space

Businesses with high staff turnover benefit from reviewing their keys more often. A master key system with restricted keys makes these changes easier and keeps unauthorized copies from circulating.
