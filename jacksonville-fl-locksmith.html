Residential Locksmith Services

House and Apartment Lockouts

Most house lockouts open cleanly with picks or bypass tools. A few won't. Some high-security locks and damaged cylinders leave no choice but to drill. If so, we'll tell you why and can fit a new lock on the same visit. Renters may need to show a lease, and now and then we'll need the landlord's OK.

Rekey or Replace?

Rekeying swaps the pins inside the lock so your old keys stop working. The hardware stays; the keys change. It almost always costs less than new locks.

Rekey when... Replace when...
You just moved in The lock is loose, worn or hard to turn
A key went missing It was forced in a break-in
A tenant or contractor had a copy You want a better deadbolt or a smart lock
You want one key for every door Rust has made it stiff

Near the Beaches and the Intracoastal, salt air wears out outdoor locks faster than people expect. If a fairly new lock has started to stick, it's often worn inside. A rekey won't fix that, so replacing it is the better spend.

Deadbolts, Repairs and Smart Locks

We install and repair deadbolts, knob and lever sets, mortise locks, sliding door locks and strike plates. We also pull broken keys out of door locks. A deadbolt is only as strong as the frame behind it. Where it helps, we set the strike plate with longer screws that reach the framing. Shopping for a new lock? Ask about its ANSI/BHMA grade. Grade 1 is the toughest. Brands we install include Schlage, Kwikset, Yale, Medeco and Mul-T-Lock.

We fit keypad, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi smart locks too, even ones you've already bought. Before you order, check that it fits your door and decide if you want a key backup.

Car Locksmith Services in Jacksonville

We work wherever your vehicle is: your driveway, the office lot or the side of the road. We handle many makes and models of cars, trucks and SUVs.

Car Lockouts

We open vehicles with proper entry tools that won't hurt the door, glass or wiring. Have your ID and registration ready. If the engine is still running with the keys inside, say so when you call.

Car Key Replacement and Programming

We cut and program keys on site, so there's no tow to the dealer. That covers spare keys, lost keys, chip keys, fobs and push-to-start keys. To quote it, we need the year, make, model and ideally the VIN, plus proof you own the car.

Some newer vehicles, mostly certain European models, need dealer-only software. If yours is one, we'll tell you on the phone before anyone drives out.

Tip: get a spare while you still have one working key. Copying a key is far simpler than an "all keys lost" job.
Key type How to spot it What replacing it involves
Plain metal key All-metal blade, older vehicles Cutting only
Transponder key Plastic head with a hidden chip Cutting plus programming the chip
Remote-head or flip key Buttons and blade in one piece Cutting, chip programming and remote pairing
Smart key Car starts with the key in your pocket Programming, plus cutting the hidden spare blade

Ignition Problems and Broken Keys

If the key won't turn or snaps off, stop forcing it. That's how ignitions get jammed. Often a worn key is the real problem, and a fresh one cut to the car's code fixes it. If not, we remove the broken piece and repair or replace the ignition.

Commercial Locksmith Services

We work with offices, shops, warehouses and rental properties, and can schedule around your hours. We also handle commercial jobs 24/7.

  • Locks and door hardware: heavy-duty commercial locks, lever sets and cylinders, installed and repaired.
  • Master key systems: staff get keys to their own doors, and managers carry one key for all of them. When someone leaves with a key, we rekey only the doors it opened.
  • Access control and high-security locks: keypad and card entry, panic bars, and pick-resistant locks with keys that can't be copied at a hardware store. Panic bars fall under fire codes, so check code questions with your property manager or fire marshal.
  • Landlords and property managers: rekeys between tenants, master-keyed buildings, mailbox locks and tenant lockouts under your rules.

We also open safes when the code or key is lost, repair safe locks, replace private mailbox locks and cut standard keys.

What a Locksmith Costs in Jacksonville

It depends on the job. A lockout costs less than a new lock, and a lost smart key costs more than a house key. Here's what moves the price:

  • Time of day. Late nights, weekends and holidays cost the same as any other time.
  • Type of job. Opening a door is quicker than fitting new hardware.
  • Lock or key type. High-security locks and smart keys take more time and pricier parts.
  • Number of locks. Rekeying is priced per lock.
  • Condition. A damaged or rusted lock may need drilling or replacing.

To us, affordable doesn't mean a lowball quote that doubles when the van shows up. It means you hear the price first and don't get sold work you don't need.
