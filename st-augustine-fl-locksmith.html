Residential Locksmith Work in St. Augustine

Getting Back Into Your Home

Most of the time, a house or condo door opens without damage using picks or bypass tools. Some high-security cylinders and broken locks may need drilling. If so, you'll hear why first, and we can fit a new lock on the same visit. More on house lockouts.

When a Rekey Makes Sense (and When It Doesn't)

Rekeying swaps out the small pins inside a lock so that every old key stops working. You keep your hardware. It's the cheap, quick fix when you've just closed on a home, misplaced a key, or a roommate moved out.

It isn't always the answer here, though. Ocean air gets into lock cylinders, and homes on Anastasia Island, in Vilano and along the beach take the worst of it. A lock that feels gritty or needs jiggling after a few years is usually corroded inside. Rekeying that one is wasted money. A new lock with a finish built for salty weather lasts longer. See rekeying and lock changes.

Old Doors, Old Hardware

Many older St. Augustine homes still have their original mortise locks, in doors cut for parts that often aren't made today. We look closely first. Plenty of old locks can be cleaned, repaired or rekeyed. When one is past saving, we find a replacement that fits the existing holes, so the wood isn't cut again. We work on mortise locks, skeleton-key (antique) locks and brands like Baldwin and Emtek.

Rental Properties and Vacation Homes

Guests forget to hand keys back. Cleaners need access. Owners and managers call us to rekey between bookings, swap worn locks, or install keypad and smart locks, so access becomes a code you change from your phone. We set the first codes before leaving. See smart lock installation and our property management service.

New Locks, Repairs and Spare Keys

Deadbolts, handle sets, sliding door locks and loose strike plates are routine, along with snapped key removal and spare keys. Shopping for a deadbolt? Check the ANSI/BHMA rating on the box. It shows how much abuse the lock was tested to take. See lock repair and installation.

Replacement Car Keys and Fobs

Lost the only key? A key cut and programmed on site saves you a tow to the dealership. We make standard keys, chip keys, remote keys, fobs and many push-to-start keys. To give you a price, we'll ask for the year, make, model, VIN and proof of ownership.

Price mostly comes down to the key. A plain metal key is just cut. A chip key also gets paired with the car's computer, and remotes and smart keys need more programming. A few newer cars, often European, need dealer-only tools. We'll tell you on the call if yours does. See car key replacement and key fob programming.

On Vacation and Lost the Rental Car Key?

It happens a lot here. Call the rental company first. Most include roadside help, and some contracts forbid outside key work. If they say yes, or you just need the door opened, we can help.

Key Stuck or Broken in the Ignition

Don't keep twisting; that's how ignition cylinders get damaged. A worn key is often the real cause, and a new one cut to the factory code can solve it. If a piece broke off inside, we extract it and repair or replace the ignition. See ignition repair.

How Pricing Works

There's no single price for "a locksmith." It depends on:

  • the hour you call, though nights and holidays cost the same as any other time
  • what the job is, because opening a door takes less time than fitting new hardware
  • the kind of lock or key, as historic, high-security and smart hardware take longer
  • how many locks you need rekeyed
  • whether the lock is in good shape or needs replacing

You'll find more on our pricing page. An affordable locksmith, in our book, quotes honestly before starting and sticks to it.
