Why Choose North Florida Locksmith in St. Augustine

We know historic hardware

St. Augustine is the oldest continuously settled European city in the continental United States, and many homes and businesses in and around the Historic District still have original mortise locks, rim locks, and antique hardware. We inspect older mechanisms carefully and repair them when possible instead of ripping them out.

Vacation rental experts

St. Augustine has a lot of short-term rentals, and keys walk off with guests all the time. We rekey between stays, install smart locks so guests get a code instead of a key, and help owners and managers keep access under control.

Coastal-ready hardware

Salt air on Anastasia Island, St. Augustine Beach, and Vilano Beach corrodes locks quickly. We recommend and install hardware that holds up better near the water.

Car keys on the spot

Tourists and locals alike lock keys in cars at the beach, downtown parking garages, and outlet malls. We come to your vehicle and cut and program replacement keys for most makes and models.

One locksmith for everything

Home, car, business, or rental, one call covers it.
