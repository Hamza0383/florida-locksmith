Why Mandarin Homeowners Call North Florida Locksmith

Rekeys for established neighborhoods

Mandarin is full of family homes that have changed hands more than once. If you've just moved in, you can't know how many keys the previous owners handed out to relatives, neighbors, cleaners, or contractors. A rekey makes every old key useless for much less than new locks, and we can match your front, back, and garage doors to a single key.

Care for riverfront and creekside homes

Homes along the St. Johns River and Julington Creek deal with constant humidity, and exterior locks feel it first. Keys get stiff, latches stop catching, and screws rust. We clean and repair what's worth saving and recommend hardware that holds up better near the water.

Upgrades for older locks

Many Mandarin homes still have the original builder-grade locks. If yours are worn or loose, we can install stronger Grade 1 deadbolts and reinforce the strike plates.

Car keys on the spot

Locked out at a shopping center on San Jose Boulevard or lost your keys at home? We come to your car and can cut and program replacement keys for most makes and models.
